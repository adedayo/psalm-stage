## Stage Troubleshooting

### Click Track Is Audible in the House PA Speakers
1. Check your audio interface's **Direct Monitor** knob/button and turn it **OFF**.
2. On macOS, open **System Settings → Accessibility → Audio** and turn **"Play stereo audio as mono"** **OFF**.
3. On your stage mixer, verify Channel 2 (Right output) is **unassigned** from the Main LR FOH bus and routed only to IEM sends.

### macOS Says "App Cannot Be Opened"
Because Psalm Stage is distributed free without a paid Apple Developer subscription:
- Run the 1-line installer (`curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adedayo/psalm-stage/main/install.sh | bash`), **or**
- Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** after your first launch attempt and click **Open Anyway**.

### Audio Glitches or Dropouts on Stage
- Always plug the laptop into AC wall power (battery mode aggressively throttles CPU frequencies).
- Engage **Live Lock** before the service starts to keep the screen awake and lock out heavy editing tabs.

### `yt-dlp` or `ffmpeg` Not Found in Import Workbench
Open **Import Workbench → Optional Toolchain Setup**, click **Re-Scan**, or click **Browse…** to point directly to the installed binary. See [Import Workbench & Song Bundles](/psalm-stage/workbench/import-and-stems/).
