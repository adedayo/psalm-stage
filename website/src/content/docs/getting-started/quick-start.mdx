title: 5-Minute Sunday Quick Start
description: Go from first launch to running a live worship multitrack with click, cues, and pad glue.

import { Steps, Aside } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

Follow these steps to test Psalm Stage at your desk or soundcheck before Sunday service.

<Steps>
1. **Launch Psalm Stage**
   Open **Psalm Stage** from Applications (macOS) or the Start Menu (Windows). You will see four workspace tabs across the top bar:
   - **Live Stage** (primary Sunday performance screen)
   - **Setlist Studio** (build and arrange Sunday service order)
   - **Loop Studio** (create custom percussion grooves and stem bundles)
   - **Import Workbench** (import local audio or YouTube songs and mark sections)

2. **Verify Your Audio Output Mode**
   In **Live Stage**, choose your audio device and routing mode:
   - **Split L/R** (default for 2-channel interfaces like Scarlett 2i2, MOTU M2, Behringer UMC202HD): **Left** carries music stems + pad + percussion; **Right** carries metronome click + vocal guide cues.
   - **Multi-Out** (for 4+ channel interfaces or X32/Dante): routes Stereo Music, Click, Cues, and Percussion onto separate hardware channels.

   <Aside type="caution">
     Before unmuting your PA speakers, verify **Direct Monitor** is **OFF** on your audio interface and macOS **"Play stereo audio as mono"** is **OFF**. See [Audio Interfaces & Stage Wiring](/psalm-stage/hardware/audio-routing/).
   </Aside>

3. **Play the Built-In Demo or Load a Song**
   Psalm Stage boots ready to audition with a built-in demo arrangement, or you can open **Setlist Studio** / **Import Workbench** to load your own stem folders.

4. **Master the Core Keyboard Shortcuts**
   | Shortcut | Action |
   | :--- | :--- |
   | `Space` | **Play / Stop** transport |
   | `L` | **Toggle Infinite Loop** on the active song section |
   | `M` | **Mute / Unmute Click** audio (visual beat dots stay active) |
   | `P` | **Toggle Ambient Tonic Pad** |
   | `1` – `9` | **Queue Section #1–#9** (jumps cleanly on the next bar or end of section) |

5. **Connect a MIDI Controller (Optional)**
   Plug in any USB MIDI keyboard or pedalboard (Morningstar, Line 6 Helix, M-Audio, Arturia, etc.). Psalm Stage connects automatically in **Omni Mode**. Click **MIDI Assign** in the header, click any button or section tile, and tap your pedal or key to bind it.
</Steps>
