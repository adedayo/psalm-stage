# Community Code of Conduct

Psalm Stage is built to serve worship teams, musicians, sound engineers, and open-source developers around the world. We are committed to maintaining a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community for everyone regardless of background or experience level.

## Expected Behavior

* Communicate with kindness, patience, and clarity in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
* Remember that many users are volunteer church musicians and audio techs rather than software engineers—write documentation and support replies accordingly.
* Offer constructive, actionable feedback on code and design proposals, and receive feedback graciously.
* Respect differing musical traditions, stage workflows, and hardware setups.

## Unacceptable Behavior

* Harassment, personal attacks, insults, or demeaning remarks.
* Discrimination or exclusionary language of any kind.
* Publishing another person's private contact details or sensitive church/system information without permission.
* Disruptive trolling, spam, or bad-faith behavior in project spaces.

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that violates these standards, please report it privately to the project maintainer (**Dr. Dayo Adetoye**) via GitHub or security/maintainer contact channels listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove off-topic or harmful comments and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
